## NotebookLM Prompt — Generate Benefit Optimizer Infographic Spec

NotebookLM can't render an image, so this prompt produces a tight, structured spec you'll hand to Canva, a designer, or an AI image tool.

Paste the block below into NotebookLM:

---

Produce a single-page vertical infographic spec for the **Benefit Optimizer**, aimed at Medicare Advantage health plan executives who own Stars, Quality, and Member Experience. Pull from the loaded sources and project direction.

**Positioning is locked. Do not drift.**

- Stars and CAHPS improvement play, not a benefit utilization play. Never use "maximizer" or any framing about capturing unused benefit dollars.
- **Needs-based smart filter**, not a clinical or condition-based tool. We are not clinicians, we do not diagnose, and we never ask for or store member diagnoses. Member-facing language stays at the level of *needs, goals, and wellness* — never *conditions, diagnoses, or clinical interventions*.
- Backend taxonomy can be sophisticated for the plan-facing ROI story (mapping needs categories to HEDIS-relevant outcomes), but member-facing surfaces are always framed as a really smart shopping filter.

**Language swaps — apply throughout:**

- Condition-based / condition-aware → needs-based / needs-aware
- Health-directed purchases → needs-aligned purchases
- Diabetes Support bundle → Blood sugar wellness essentials
- Fall prevention & diabetes care → Mobility support, daily wellness
- Clinical intervention / health intervention → wellness support / smart needs filter

**Infographic structure, top to bottom:**

1. **Headline block** — short headline plus a one-line subhead. Lead with the Star Ratings outcome. Do not use "condition-aware," "health-directed," or any diagnosis-implying language in the headline.
2. **The shift** — two-column From / To. From: reactive, random, unguided catalog. To: smart needs filter, guided, signal-rich.
3. **Three pillars** — three icons in a row with 3-word titles and one-sentence descriptions: Smart Needs-Based Carts; Reminders & Engagement; Feedback Loop. Bundle examples shown must use needs-based names (e.g., "Mobility & daily wellness essentials"), never diagnosis names.
4. **Star Ratings impact map** — grid mapping each pillar to CAHPS domains (customer service, ease of getting care, rating of plan) and to plan-facing outcome categories. Use category labels like "mobility support," "daily wellness," "medication routine support" — not diagnosis labels.
5. **Member journey strip** — 4-step horizontal flow: needs intake (no PHI) → smart cart → quarterly reminder → feedback captured. Each step's caption must use needs/goals language only.
6. **Plan ROI stack** — four short stat-style call-outs: Stars lift, retention/NPS, fulfillment efficiency, smarter utilization.
7. **Footer** — one-line CTA for a pilot conversation with the plan's Stars team for the 2027 plan year.

For each section provide: headline text (max 8 words), supporting copy (max 25 words), visual suggestion (icon, layout cue, color role). Suggest a two-color palette plus one accent — calm and trustworthy, not clinical or retail. Total on-page word count under 200. Flag any source content that drifts into condition-based, diagnosis-implying, or utilization-first language and rewrite it.
