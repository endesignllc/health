## NotebookLM Prompt — Generate Benefit Optimizer Deck

Paste the block below into NotebookLM as your prompt:

---

Build a 10–12 slide pitch deck for the **Benefit Optimizer**, aimed at Medicare Advantage health plan executives who own Stars, Quality, and Member Experience. Pull from the loaded sources and project direction.

**Positioning is locked. Do not drift.**

- This is a Stars and CAHPS improvement play, not a benefit utilization play. Never use "maximizer" or any framing about capturing unused benefit dollars.
- This is a **needs-based smart filter**, not a clinical or condition-based tool. We are not clinicians, we do not diagnose, and we never ask for or store member diagnoses. Member-facing language stays at the level of *needs, goals, and wellness* — never *conditions, diagnoses, or clinical interventions*.
- The backend taxonomy can be sophisticated (mapping needs categories to HEDIS-relevant outcomes for the plan-facing ROI story), but the member experience is always framed as a really smart shopping filter that helps members find products that fit their everyday needs.

**Language swaps — apply throughout:**

- Condition-based / condition-aware (member-facing) → needs-based / needs-aware
- Health-directed purchases → needs-aligned purchases
- Diabetes Support bundle → Blood sugar wellness essentials
- Fall prevention & diabetes care → Mobility support, daily wellness
- Clinical intervention / health intervention → wellness support / smart needs filter
- "Guide members toward health-directed purchases" → "Help members find products that fit their everyday needs"

**Slide structure:**

1. **Title** — Benefit Optimizer: turning the OTC benefit into a Star Ratings lever.
2. **The problem** — OTC benefits today are reactive and unguided. Members are overwhelmed; plans get no signal back.
3. **The shift** — From random catalog browsing to a needs-based, guardrailed experience.
4. **What it is** — One-paragraph description plus a visual of the smart-filter cart experience. Emphasize needs-based, no PHI requested.
5. **Pillar 1: Smart needs-based carts** — bundles built around member-stated needs (e.g., mobility support, daily wellness, sleep, hydration). No diagnoses asked or stored.
6. **Pillar 2: Reminders & engagement** — quarterly cadence, hold-and-ship, proactive nudges keeping members connected to the plan.
7. **Pillar 3: Feedback loop** — every interaction generates structured signal the plan can act on for Stars and member experience.
8. **Star Ratings impact** — map pillars to CAHPS domains (customer service, ease of getting care, rating of plan) and to HEDIS-relevant outcome categories at the **plan-facing taxonomy level**, not as member-facing bundle names.
9. **Member experience** — short narrative walkthrough of a member's quarter using needs/goal language only.
10. **Plan ROI** — Stars lift, retention/NPS, fulfillment efficiency, smarter utilization (not more).
11. **How it fits** — standalone site, fulfillment via Medline, GTIN-based catalog layer; no B2B2C burden on the plan; no PHI handling.
12. **Next step** — pilot conversation with the plan's Stars team for the 2027 plan year.

Tone: clinical-credibility for the plan audience, but **never clinical in member-facing copy**. 5–8 word headlines, 2–4 supporting bullets per slide, one visual suggestion per slide. Flag any source content that drifts back into condition-based, diagnosis-implying, or utilization-first framing and rewrite it.
