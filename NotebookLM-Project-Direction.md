## What it is
A curated, condition-aware OTC platform that helps Medicare Advantage plans lift CAHPS and Star ratings by guiding members to the right products, reminding them to engage, and feeding member signal back to the plan.

## Three pillars (co-equal)
- **Curated, condition-based carts** — bundles tied to HEDIS categories (fall prevention, diabetes, pain/inflammation). Conditions filter; no PHI disclosure.
- **Reminders & engagement** — quarterly cadence, hold-and-ship, proactive nudges keeping members connected to the plan.
- **Feedback loop** — every interaction generates structured signal plans can act on for Stars and care management.

## Audience & tone
Default audience: plan execs owning Stars, Quality, Member Experience. Tone: clinical, Stars-fluent. Lead with quality outcomes, not shopping.

## ROI framing (in order)
1. Star Ratings lift via CAHPS and HEDIS
2. Member retention / NPS
3. Single-fulfillment-partner efficiency
4. Smarter utilization, not more utilization

## Hard guardrails
- Never use "maximizer" or any framing about capturing unused dollars or spending the full allowance — plan execs read that as margin erosion.
- Never frame as a discount catalog, generic marketplace, or B2B2C tech stack.
- Never lead with utilization numbers.

## Language swaps
- Maximizer → optimizer / guide
- Capture unused dollars → direct spend toward health-improving products
- Spend more → spend on purpose
- Upsell → bundle for outcomes
